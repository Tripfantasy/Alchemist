**Overview for collaborators** · Built 2026-11-04

> We study how the heart and other organs repair themselves after injury, from
> the molecular programs that switch on in regenerating cells to the tissue
> architecture they rebuild. Most of our work pairs single-cell and spatial
> genomics with physiology and imaging in mouse injury models.

Cardiac regeneration is the lab's largest theme, followed by disease modeling,
tissue architecture and gene regulation. Most questions are answered with bulk
or single-cell RNA-seq, alongside physiology, imaging, proteomics and behavior.

## 2. Methods beyond sequencing

Spatial transcriptomics, patch-clamp electrophysiology, calcium imaging,
confocal microscopy, in situ hybridization, proteomics and behavior.

## 3. Biological focus

- **Tissues:** heart (left ventricle, cardiomyocytes), kidney, liver, lung, cortex.
- **Stages and timepoints:** mostly time after injury or treatment, with some
  neonatal and adult work.
- **Conditions and models:** myocardial infarction, injury, high-fat diet.

## 4. Toolkit

- **Genetic tools:** CreERT2 driver, tdTomato reporter, GCaMP indicator.
- **Analysis languages:** Python, R, MATLAB.
